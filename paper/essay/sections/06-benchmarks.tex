\section{Benchmark Metrics}\label{sec:bench}
\ph{Main idea: benchmark setup and units. Then, in order: all measurements target
the size-06 digest and the keyed-MD5 construction; the host has 10 logical cores;
rates are millions of keys hashed per second (Mkeys/s). Close on: these units
apply to \autoref{tab:bench} and the appendix scaling sweep.}

\paragraph{CPU-bound vs.\ network-bound.} \ph{Main idea: the workload is
overwhelmingly compute-bound, so coordination is not the bottleneck. Then, in
order: each worker's wall time splits into hashing versus time blocked on the
socket; across the distributed run aggregate compute time was $69.1$\,s against
$0.03$\,s of socket wait, i.e.\ 99.96\% of time spent hashing; throughput
therefore scales with CPU cores until the host saturates, as in \autoref{tab:bench}
where per-core rate falls once the process count exceeds the physical-core
budget. Close on: the TCP coordination protocol is not the bottleneck, CPU is.}

\paragraph{End-to-end.} \ph{Main idea: end-to-end recovery time, single-threaded
versus cluster. Then, in order: full recovery of \texttt{ykusrq} took $738$\,s
single-threaded versus $142$\,s on the contended cluster; an uncontended
$\sim$12.6\,Mkeys/s cluster covers the whole $2.18\times10^9$ space in well under
that. Close on: the six-character base-36 space is a minutes-scale target at
cluster rates.}
